Nahari — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Version 1.0
In one line: Nahari runs entirely on your device. It collects no data, sends no data, and does not even request the Internet permission.

1. Who we are

  • App: Nahari — a personal companion for routines, habits, reminders and tasks.
  • Package name: xyz.orcanox.nahari
  • Developer: Orcanox — Nabil Karem.
  • Contact: nabilkarem@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

None. No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, no third-party data collection.

3. Where your data lives

  • Everything you enter (habits, reminders, tasks, journal, settings) is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database.
  • The encryption key is generated on your device and stored in the Android Keystore.
  • There is no server, no cloud backup and no sync. The app has no Internet permission, so it cannot transmit anything.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionPurpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow the reminder notification on time.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMSchedule the reminder at the exact minute.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-schedule reminders after a device restart.
VIBRATEVibrate with the notification.
WAKE_LOCKWake the device briefly so the reminder fires on time.
INTERNETNot required — and not present in the app.

5. Data sharing

None. No data leaves your device, and nothing is shared with any third party.

6. Data deletion

  • Any item can be deleted inside the app (with a recycle bin and permanent delete).
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data, since it exists only on your device.

7. Children

Nahari is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from any user of any age.

8. Security

The database is encrypted on-device with a key held in the platform secure store. Because all data is local, backing up your device is your responsibility — we hold no copy to restore.

9. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
